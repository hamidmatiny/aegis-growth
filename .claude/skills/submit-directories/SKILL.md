# Submit Directories

## Purpose

Get AEGIS listed on relevant **free** directories. No paid ads, no cold outreach.

## Allowed targets (extend with evidence)

- G2, Capterra, SaaSHub, alternative.to / similar open catalogs, curated security-tool lists that accept vendor submissions

## Excluded

- Paid placement
- Personal/cold outreach
- Community spam (Reddit/LinkedIn/HN) — route through `aegis-ceo` if ever pursued

## Process

### Step 1: Read tracker

Open `memory/directory-submissions.md`. Skip already-submitted / rejected.

### Step 2: Prepare listing packet

Canonical facts only:

- Name: AEGIS / defenseaegis.org
- Category: LLM/agent security, SMB security copilot
- Pricing honesty: state real public pricing if known; never invent MRR or customer counts

### Step 3: Submit or queue

If the form can be completed with available credentials/browser: submit and record confirmation id/URL. If human CAPTCHA/login required: prepare the packet and escalate to Hamid via CEO with exact form URL — do not claim submitted.

### Step 4: Update tracker + Slack

Append status. Close out in `#aegis-growth`.

## Final step — Slack close-out (mandatory)
